Getting a clean reading
Rest your finger flat on the sensor with light pressure — pressing hard squeezes the blood out. Cover both lights. Sit still and breathe normally.

Blood pressure

FROM YOUR CUFF

Sit quietly for five minutes first, arm at heart height. This is the most important number in pregnancy and no wearable can measure it.

←

Temperature

FROM A THERMOMETER

The belt measures the air around you, not your body. A thermometer under the tongue or arm is the only reading that counts here.

←

Weight

ONCE A WEEK, SAME TIME

Morning, before eating, same scales each time. A sudden jump usually means fluid rather than fat.

←

Iron tablet

IFA · DAILY

Iron and folic acid tablets are the main defence against anaemia in pregnancy. Missing days is common and worth knowing about.

←

Lab result

HAEMOGLOBIN FROM YOUR REPORT

Copy the haemoglobin value from your blood test. Below 11 g/dL is anaemia in pregnancy.

←

Fluid leak

ANY AMOUNT COUNTS

It is hard to tell amniotic fluid from urine at home, and that is fine — only an examination can say. Report it and let someone check.

If it is a gush, or the fluid is green or bloody, or you also feel feverish — go to hospital rather than waiting.

Pulse and oxygen come from an calibrated MAX30100 worn at home. Temperature and humidity are from a DHT11.

Take a belt reading?

This runs the sensor for two minutes and records your pulse. You can stop at any time.

What gets recorded
Your pulse, an uncalibrated oxygen trend, and the air temperature and humidity under the belt. Nothing else.
What it will not do
It cannot measure your body temperature, your blood pressure, or your baby. It cannot tell you that you are well.
